How to Use

  1. Choose a mosaic and drag over it to select the starting image.
  2. Pick a module, set its parameters and click Preview Step.
  3. Click Add to Pipeline: its output becomes the next step's input.
  4. Move, edit or remove steps below; the pipeline re-runs from the start.
  5. Use Arithmetic / combine to mix an earlier stage back in, e.g. cut the object out of the original with a segmentation mask.
  6. Click Finish to review, evaluate and save the result. Load Example shows a complete worked pipeline.
